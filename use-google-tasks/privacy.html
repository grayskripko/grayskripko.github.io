use-google-tasks: privacy policy

use-google-tasks is a personal tool used only by its owner with his own Google account.

  • It accesses Google Tasks data (task lists and tasks) only to show them to the owner and to create or complete tasks at his request.
  • The data and the access token are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to third parties, and none is used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings: myaccount.google.com/connections.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
